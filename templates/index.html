About

Data, APIs and AI, built to run on their own.

I'm a Software Engineering graduate (University of Lahore, 2024) and Python developer with 2+ years of experience across data engineering, backend APIs, automation and applied AI. My strongest production experience is at a UK-based competitive-intelligence company, where I build and run the pipelines that collect product, pricing and offer data from retailer and telecom websites across multiple European markets.

I build the full system, not just one piece: REST APIs with FastAPI, Flask and Django; scrapers that handle JavaScript-heavy sites and token-authenticated private APIs; validation and cleaning with pandas; Docker deployments on AWS, GCP and Azure; and monitoring through dashboards and n8n alerts, so failures are caught before anyone downstream notices.

On the AI side I build LLM-powered agents and automation workflows (n8n, LangChain, RAG), plus computer vision systems with YOLO and MediaPipe. I like turning manual, repetitive work into reliable, automated systems.
